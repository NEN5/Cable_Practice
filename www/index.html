Fanny trainer

Touch the target to score a point. Steel Cables fly through targets and anchor on walls. Cast two cables to steer between them; older cables are ignored. Avoid the red drifters. Targets have HP: gold 1 hit, orange 3, purple 5. Touching hits once, Cut Throat hits hard. Chain kills for a streak bonus; it resets if you stop, hit a wall, get hit, or have no active cable. Each target is randomly still or moving.

Move: joystick. Swipe anywhere on the arena to fire a cable that way, or tap to fire toward the tap. Cables have no cooldown, only energy. Cut Throat: tap its button. Keys: WASD, Q cable, E Cut Throat.
